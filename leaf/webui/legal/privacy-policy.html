Privacy Policy

Application: bob (Discord bot) · Operated by Binserv · Effective 2026-06-19

This Privacy Policy explains what data the bob Discord application ("the Service") collects, how it is used, and your choices. By using the Service you agree to this policy.

1. What we collect

We process only what is needed to answer you:

  • Message content you direct to the bot — a message that @-mentions the bot, or a direct message to it. We do not read other channel messages (the bot runs without Discord's privileged Message Content intent).
  • Attachments you send to the bot (e.g. images for OCR), and any text extracted from them.
  • Discord identifiers — your user ID and display name, and the message, channel, and server (guild) IDs needed to route a reply.

2. How we use it

Solely to operate the Service: to understand your request, generate a response, and reply in the same conversation. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Third-party AI providers

To generate replies, message content (and extracted attachment text) may be sent to third-party or self-hosted AI model providers (for example OpenRouter and self-hosted models). Their handling of that content is governed by their own privacy policies. Do not send secrets, credentials, or sensitive personal data you do not want processed this way.

4. Retention

Conversation history is retained to give the assistant context for follow-ups, for up to 30 days, after which it is eligible for deletion. You can ask the operator to delete your data sooner (see Contact).

5. Storage & security

Data is stored on infrastructure controlled by Binserv and protected with reasonable technical measures. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Your choices

You can stop using the Service at any time (remove the bot or stop messaging it). To request access to, or deletion of, the data associated with your Discord user ID, contact us below.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to anyone under the minimum age required by Discord in their country (at least 13). We do not knowingly collect data from such users.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Effective" date above reflects the latest version. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests: agent@binserv.us.
